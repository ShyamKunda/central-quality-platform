ANATOMY OF A SIGNAL

What each field tells us

subject

What is this about?

A content digest joins evidence across commit, build, artifact, deployment, and production.

producer

Who observed it?

The tool, version, and run link make the claim traceable.

measurement

What was measured?

A typed verdict, ratio, count, threshold, delta, or distribution—not a lossy universal number.

outcome

What did the tool conclude?

Pass, fail, warning, error, skipped, or pending describes the technical result.

trust

Can it block?

Active checks may veto. Quarantined checks remain visible but temporarily lose their veto.

freshness

Does it still apply?

Occurrence, receipt, and expiry timestamps reveal late or stale evidence.

WHY ABSENCE MATTERS

“No result” is not one state.

A pending check may resolve itself. A check that never ran means the pipeline is broken. A producer outage invokes that rule’s explicit fail-open or fail-closed behavior.

PendingWait for its deadline
MissingRepair the pipeline
ExpiredCollect fresh evidence
Producer outageApply fail-open or fail-closed policy
